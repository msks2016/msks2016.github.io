About Me

I’m an MSc student in Oncology at Shanghai University, working on non-coding RNAs in lung cancer through both wet-lab experiments and bioinformatics analysis. With a background in biochemistry and clinical laboratory work, I have co-authored eight published articles in oncology, infectious disease, ophthalmology, nutrition and environmental health.

I’m building my skills in bioinformatics and scientific writing, and this website is where I share my work. If you’re a professor or an employer, you can find my research, my publications and my CV here.

I’m looking for research, higher-study and career opportunities in bioinformatics and medical and scientific writing. If you have a question or an idea for a collaboration, feel free to reach out.

Research

Interests: ncRNA and cancer biology · bioinformatics for cancer research · pharmaceutical biotechnology · fragment-based drug design

Benchmarking published lncRNA/miRNA prognostic signatures in lung adenocarcinoma in progress

Independent computational project · Shanghai University · manuscript in preparation

  • Systematic PubMed search and screening of published signatures, with structured data extraction and a reporting-completeness survey.
  • Scoring of selected signatures in independent cohorts (TCGA-LUAD, CPTAC-3, GEO) in R, using survival analysis and random-signature comparisons.
  • Analysis plan registered on OSF; code is version-controlled on GitHub.
