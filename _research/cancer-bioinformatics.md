I analyse public cancer datasets such as TCGA, CPTAC-3 and GEO in R, with a focus on survival analysis and on how prognostic models are built, reported and validated. I try to make this work transparent and reproducible: pre-registering analysis plans on the Open Science Framework, following PRISMA-style procedures for literature screening and data extraction, and keeping analysis code under version control on GitHub.
