My current research asks how long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) and microRNAs contribute to lung adenocarcinoma, the most common type of lung cancer. I am particularly interested in competing endogenous RNA (ceRNA) networks, in which lncRNAs bind microRNAs and so change the expression of protein-coding genes. I work from public transcriptomic data towards hypotheses that can be tested in the laboratory, using methods such as cell culture, transfection, qRT-PCR, western blotting, luciferase reporter assays and proliferation assays.

Beyond cancer, I am also reviewing what is known about lncRNAs in foods and in the body's response to diet.
