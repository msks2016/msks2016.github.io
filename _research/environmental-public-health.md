Before moving to Shanghai, I contributed to public-health and environmental-health studies in Bangladesh, including government-funded projects with the 4-Green Research Society. These studies examined endocrine-disrupting chemicals (bisphenol A in women with breast cancer, and an estrogenic compound in drinking water), child and adolescent health (myopia, nutrition and respiratory infections), antibiotic-resistant bacteria in street food, and COVID-19 outcomes. They combined laboratory measurements such as ELISA with surveys, interviews and clinical records.
